About the recordings, your practice set, and this project

Which version am I hearing?

Book recording means the artist named in the printed TOC. Earlier / original identifies an earlier version when research supports it; it does not imply that every first performance has been traced. Ukulele version is a separate performance or lesson, not a promise that it matches this book's arrangement.

Videos were researched on October 28, 2026. Titles and performers were checked against search results and video metadata where accessible. Embedding and playback can change. Every video has a direct YouTube link and every song has a fresh search link.

A listening companion

This independent project links recordings; it does not reproduce the book's music, tablature, or lyrics. It is not affiliated with Hal Leonard or the recording artists.

Your practice set, heard marks, and notes stay in this browser on this device. They do not sync between devices. Download a backup if you want to keep them.
